Arctos Labs is a premium sports nutrition and wellness brand built on a single promise: **every claim is substantiated and every lot is verifiable.** Our packaging carries a QR code linking to the third-party certificate of analysis for that specific lot. Our customers are expected to scan it.

## 2. NON-NEGOTIABLE STANDARDS

### 2.1 Facility and compliance
- FDA-registered facility, current registration number to be provided.
- cGMP compliance under **21 CFR Part 111** (dietary supplements) — not merely food GMP or tobacco-alternative GMP.
- Third-party GMP certification (NSF, NPA, UL, or equivalent) — certificate to be provided.
- Written recall procedure and mock recall records available for review.
- Allergen control program documented. Facility allergen status to be disclosed per SKU.

### 2.2 Label transparency — absolute requirements
1. **No proprietary blends.** Every active ingredient is disclosed with its exact milligram amount. No exceptions, no "matrix," no "complex."
2. **Elemental amounts declared for all minerals.** Magnesium, zinc, potassium, calcium, etc. are declared as elemental content, not compound weight.
3. **Standardization declared** for every botanical extract (e.g., "milk thistle extract, 80% silymarin"; "beetroot extract, standardized to 15% nitrate").
4. **Extract ratios and plant part declared** for all botanicals.
5. **Beta-glucan content declared** for all mushroom ingredients — *not* "polysaccharides." See 2.4.
6. **No fairy dusting.** If we cannot include a clinically meaningful dose of an ingredient, we remove the ingredient. Please do not propose formulas that add sub-clinical amounts of trendy actives for label appeal. We will reject them.

### 2.3 Excipients and prohibited inputs
**Prohibited in all products:**
- Artificial sweeteners: sucralose, aspartame, acesulfame potassium, saccharin
- Maltodextrin, dextrose, or any caloric/glycemic filler used as bulk
- Artificial colors and dyes (FD&C anything), titanium dioxide
- Artificial flavors
- Hydrogenated oils, trans fats
- Sugar alcohols in pouches (acceptable in limited use in effervescent tablets only, with prior written approval)
- Magnesium stearate where a clean alternative exists; if used, disclose and justify
- Talc, silicon dioxide above functional minimum
- PEGs, propylene glycol, BHA/BHT
- Any ingredient sourced from a supplier who cannot provide a full CoA and country of origin

**Permitted sweeteners/flavors:** monk fruit extract (preferred), stevia (Reb M only, no Reb A bitterness), natural flavors with full disclosure of components on request, natural essential oils, allulose (powders only, with approval).

**Capsules:** vegetarian HPMC preferred. Bovine gelatin acceptable where specified. No titanium dioxide in shells.

### 2.4 Mushroom ingredient specification (applies to all mushroom products)
- **Fruiting body only.** Mycelium-on-grain is prohibited outright.
- Minimum **25% beta-glucan** by validated enzymatic assay (Megazyme or equivalent). "Polysaccharide" figures that include alpha-glucan/starch are not acceptable as a specification.
- Species identity verified by DNA or equivalent method.
- Starch content tested and reported (must be under 5%).
- Country of origin disclosed; heavy metals tested per lot.

### 2.5 Third-party testing — the core of our brand
For **every production lot**, of **every SKU**, we require third-party (not in-house) testing at an ISO 17025-accredited laboratory for:

| Test | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Identity | All actives confirmed |
| Potency / label claim | Every declared active assayed; within ±10% of label, never below 100% of claim at release |
| Heavy metals | Lead, arsenic, cadmium, mercury — must meet California Prop 65 thresholds without a warning label |
| Microbiological | Total plate count, yeast/mold, E. coli, Salmonella, S. aureus |
| Residual solvents | Where extracts are used |
| Pesticides | Botanical and mushroom ingredients |
| Allergen verification | Where cross-contact risk exists |
| Banned substances | See 2.6 |
| Product-specific | See individual SKU specs (e.g., measured H2 output; creatine DCD/dicyandiamide and creatinine) |

CoAs must be delivered in a format we can publish publicly, with lot number, test dates, laboratory name, and accreditation. **We publish these. Please confirm you are comfortable with that.**

### 2.6 Banned-substance program
Our target customers include tested athletes, military, and law enforcement. We require:
- Per-lot screening against the WADA prohibited list for all sports products.
- A roadmap to **Informed Sport** or **NSF Certified for Sport** certification within 12–18 months. Please indicate whether your facility is already certified under either program, and if not, what would be required.
- Written assurance that no facility line producing our SKUs also handles prohormones, SARMs, DMAA, DMHA, or other adulterants. **If your facility manufactures those products, we cannot work with you.**

### 2.7 Stability and shelf life
- Real-time and accelerated stability data required for every new formula prior to first commercial run.
- Minimum 24-month shelf life target for powders and capsules; 18 months for pouches; 24 months for tablets.
- Moisture content specifications and water activity data for pouches and effervescent tablets.

## 4. TIER 2 PRODUCTS — ATHLETE ORGAN & RECOVERY SUPPORT

### 4.1 Positioning statement — please read before formulating

This line is positioned as **general athlete organ and recovery support**, marketed to the broad population of hard-training adults, heavy-training masters athletes, people with high alcohol or NSAID exposure, and anyone whose bloodwork warrants attention. It is **not** marketed as "cycle support," and we will not use that language, imply protection from any drug, or reference performance-enhancing drug use in marketing.

That decision is deliberate and firm, for four reasons: the addressable market is far larger; ad platforms and payment processors restrict steroid-adjacent marketing; "cycle support" framing invites regulatory scrutiny of intended use; and most importantly, **a supplement that makes someone feel protected while taking a higher risk causes net harm.** No formula or label in this line may imply organ protection.

All formulas below are legal dietary ingredients dosed at or near studied amounts.

|---|---|---|

| Calcium-D-glucarate | 1,000 mg | Beta-glucuronidase inhibition — mechanistic, human data limited |
| Grape seed extract (procyanidin-standardized) | 300 mg | In-vitro aromatase activity; weak human data |

| **Pyridoxal-5-phosphate (P5P)** | **25 mg maximum** | See below |
| Boron | 6 mg | |

**⚠️ IMPORTANT — B6 dose is capped deliberately.** We were initially considering a high-dose P5P product. We are not doing that. Vitamin B6 in supplemental form causes peripheral sensory neuropathy, and the regulatory picture has tightened considerably: Australia's TGA found neuropathy can occur at daily doses under 50 mg and in people taking multiple B6-containing products, and now requires a neuropathy warning on any product exceeding **10 mg** per daily dose, having concluded no minimum safe dose, duration, or risk-factor profile has been established. Nerve damage may not fully resolve after stopping. Given that our customers commonly stack multiple products, a high-dose B6 product is an unacceptable risk to them and to us.

**Therefore:** P5P capped at 25 mg/day, and this product carries the neuropathy warning on-label regardless of jurisdiction:
> *WARNING — Contains vitamin B6. Stop use and consult a healthcare practitioner if you experience tingling, burning, or numbness in the hands or feet. Do not use with other vitamin B6-containing supplements without medical advice.*

**Mandatory label language for this SKU:**
> *This product supports normal estrogen metabolism. It is not an aromatase inhibitor and is not a substitute for medical evaluation or treatment. Consult a physician about any breast tissue changes, lumps, or tenderness.*
