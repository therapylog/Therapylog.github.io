# ARCTOS LABS — DEN (PM) & RITUAL (AM) Formulation Specs
*Evidence-graded ingredient specifications · current as of August 2026*

---

## How to read this document

Every ingredient is graded so you know which ones your claims can rest on and which are there for the ritual:

- **A** = multiple human RCTs at the dose specified. Claims can lean here.
- **B** = real human data, smaller or mixed. Supporting cast.
- **C** = mechanistically plausible, thin human evidence. Include for consumer expectation/experience only — never headline it.

A brand whose whole promise is "verified" cannot afford to headline a C-grade ingredient. That's the trap most of your competitors are already in, and it's your opening.

# PART I — THE DEN SYSTEM (PM / Wind-Down)

Three formats, one flavor language, designed to be used together or separately:

| Format | Role | Key constraint |
|---|---|---|
| **DEN Pouch** | Fast onset under the lip, 15–30 min pre-bed | ~350 mg active budget — micro-dose actives only |
| **DEN Nightcap** (drink powder) | The full clinical dose + the ritual of sipping | No weight limit — this is where the real formula lives |
| **DEN Capsules** | Travel / take-with-a-shake convenience | ~1.4 g total across 2 caps — no gram-scale actives |

---

## 1 — DEN NIGHTCAP · Nighttime Relaxation Drink

**This is the flagship of the PM line, not the pouch.** Because sleep's best-evidenced ingredients require *grams*, only a drink can deliver a genuinely effective dose. The pouch is the fast-onset companion; the powder is the product that actually works.

### Formula (per ~12 g serving stick or scoop)

| Ingredient | Dose | Grade | Why it's in |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Glycine** | 3 g | **A** | The single best-evidenced sleep ingredient nobody uses. RCTs show 3 g before bed shortens sleep-onset latency, improves subjective sleep quality, and reduces next-day fatigue — while *preserving* normal sleep architecture, unlike hypnotics. Works partly by lowering core body temperature. Naturally sweet, dissolves clean, dirt cheap. **This ingredient is the product.** |
| **Magnesium bisglycinate** | 200 mg elemental | **A** | The 2025 Schuster RCT (n=155) used 250 mg elemental bisglycinate and improved insomnia severity scores. Note carefully: label weight ≠ elemental — a "500 mg magnesium glycinate" often contains ~70 mg elemental. **Declare elemental on the panel.** Bisglycinate also donates additional glycine and has the lowest GI/laxative rate. |
| **L-Theanine** | 200 mg | **A** | Alpha-wave promotion, anxiolytic without sedation, extremely clean safety record. 200 mg is the studied dose (your pouch uses 100 mg — the drink goes full). |
| **Montmorency tart cherry extract** | 500 mg (or 60:1 concentrate equiv.) | **B** | Contains naturally occurring melatonin and procyanidins; small RCTs show improved sleep time/efficiency. Doubles as a flavor and color asset, and it's an *athlete-credible* ingredient (also studied for exercise recovery/DOMS) — perfect crossover for your gym channel. |
| **Melatonin** | 0.5 mg | **A** (for onset) | **Deliberately low.** 0.3–1 mg is the physiologic range; higher doses aren't better for onset and cause next-day grogginess. "0.5 mg, not 10" is a premium-signalling *marketing* line for the informed audience that has been burned by gas-station gummies. Make it optional: see the melatonin-free SKU note below. |
| **Lemon balm** (*Melissa officinalis*, standardized) | 300 mg | **B** | Genuine human anxiolytic data, traditional evening use, and it tastes good in a tea format. Better-supported than most "calm" botanicals. |
| **Saffron extract** (affron® or equivalent, 3.5% lepticrosalides) | 28 mg | **B** | Has actual sleep-quality RCT data at 28 mg, plus mood data. Tiny dose, premium branded-ingredient story, supplier dossier for your substantiation file. |
| **Apigenin** | 50 mg | **C** | Popular with the Huberman audience, so consumers expect it. Include for that reason, dosed honestly, but **never headline it** — the evidence in humans is thin. |
| **Zinc bisglycinate** | 7 mg elemental | **C** | Rounds out the "ZMA" expectation for the athlete audience at a sane dose. Optional. |

**Base/flavor system:** monk fruit + a touch of glycine's own sweetness (glycine is mildly sweet — a real formulation advantage), natural flavors, citric acid where needed, no maltodextrin, no sucralose, no artificial color.

### Deliberately excluded — and why this matters for you specifically

- **5-HTP and L-tryptophan.** Both raise serotonin and carry serotonin-syndrome interaction risk with SSRIs/SNRIs/MAOIs. Given your adjacency to a mental-health app, a meaningful share of your audience is on psychiatric medication. Including these would be both a liability exposure and, frankly, the wrong thing to do. **Hard exclude.**
- **St. John's Wort.** Massive CYP450 interaction profile (SSRIs, birth control, anticoagulants). Hard exclude for the same reason.
- **Ashwagandha.** Judgment call, and I'd leave it out of a *nightly* product. Large RCTs at 300 mg twice daily of standardized root extract show no clinically significant liver-enzyme changes, but there is now a real published case literature of ashwagandha-associated liver injury — systematic reviews using RUCAM causality scoring find mostly "probable" cases, and while most resolved on discontinuation, **several cases resulted in death or transplant**. For a product designed for nightly indefinite use by an audience that may be stacking other supplements, that risk/benefit doesn't clear the bar for a brand whose entire promise is clean verification. If you want it later, it belongs in a *cycled* daytime stress SKU (root extract only, 8-week-on/4-off, explicit liver warning), not in DEN.
- **GABA.** Poor blood-brain-barrier penetration; effects are largely peripheral/placebo. It's in half the sleep products on the market, which is exactly why leaving it out is a talking point.
- **Valerian.** Mixed evidence and an aggressively unpleasant smell that would wreck a premium flavor system.
- **High-dose melatonin (3–10 mg).** Actively worse for onset and causes grogginess. Position against it.

### Two SKUs recommended

1. **DEN NIGHTCAP** — full formula with 0.5 mg melatonin.
2. **DEN NIGHTCAP · MELATONIN-FREE** — identical minus melatonin. Real demand exists (nightly-use hesitancy, people who wake groggy, shift workers, teen athletes) and it costs you nothing but a second flavor run. Competitors rarely offer this. It also gives trainers an answer for clients who "can't do melatonin."

### Flavor architecture (answering your coffee/tea question)

You asked for coffee or tea minus the caffeine. Best options, ranked:

| Flavor | Notes |
|---|---|
| **NIGHT CACAO** ⭐ | Dutch-process cocoa + a whisper of sea salt. The "hot chocolate for adults" ritual is the single most natural nighttime drink behavior. One caveat to verify with your co-man: cacao carries **theobromine**, a mild stimulant. Use alkalized cocoa powder at flavor levels (not high-theobromine cacao extract) and it's a non-issue for most people — but test it and consider a "contains naturally occurring theobromine" line. |
| **ROASTED CHICORY** ⭐ | Roasted chicory + dandelion root is the classic caffeine-free coffee substitute (New Orleans coffee tradition) — genuinely coffee-like, zero caffeine, and inulin fiber adds body. This is your "evening coffee" answer and it's a better story than decaf, because decaf still contains residual caffeine. |
| **HONEY MINT** | **Match this one to the DEN pouch flavor** so the stack tastes coherent — pop the pouch, sip the cup, same profile. This is the SKU your "pouch + drink together" marketing runs on. |
| **TOASTED RICE & ROOIBOS** | Genmaicha-adjacent, nutty, naturally caffeine-free rooibos base. The refined/quiet option. Lower priority — launch third. |

Launch with **Night Cacao + Honey Mint**. Chicory third.

## 3 — DEN CAPSULES

**Be upfront about the constraint:** 3 g of glycine is roughly six large capsules on its own, so capsules physically cannot replicate the Nightcap. Two honest ways to build this:

**Option A (recommended) — "DEN NIGHT CAPS," 2 capsules, no glycine:**
- Magnesium bisglycinate 200 mg elemental
- L-Theanine 200 mg
- Melatonin 0.5 mg
- Lemon balm 300 mg
- Saffron 28 mg
- Apigenin 50 mg

Positioned as *travel and convenience* — "the Nightcap without the mug." Add a line to the label: "For full glycine dosing, use DEN NIGHTCAP." That transparency builds more trust than pretending parity.

**Option B — a two-piece system:** DEN Night Caps (above) + a separate **unflavored glycine scoop** (pure glycine, 3 g, mildly sweet, mixes into anything — including a protein shake, which is exactly what you described). Glycine as a standalone single-ingredient product also fits your "one ingredient, nothing else" creatine platform perfectly and costs almost nothing to produce.

Option B is more product SKUs but a *much* better story, and the standalone glycine tub is a legitimately differentiated item almost nobody sells well.

# PART II — THE AM RITUAL LINE (Coffee & Matcha)

## 4 — ARCTOS RITUAL · Mushroom Coffee

### The quality angle that wins this category

Most mushroom coffee is bad in a specific, provable way: it uses **mycelium grown on grain**, which means the powder is largely starch, and it's labeled by "polysaccharides" — a number that *counts that starch*. Your spec should be the opposite, and you should say so loudly:

> **Fruiting body only. No mycelium-on-grain. Beta-glucans tested and declared — not "polysaccharides."**

That single sentence, backed by your per-lot COA QR, is the most defensible differentiation available in the mushroom category. Require ≥25–30% beta-glucan on the extract COA.

### Formula (per ~6 g serving)

| Ingredient | Dose | Grade | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Freeze-dried arabica instant coffee | ~2 g (≈90–100 mg caffeine) | A | Specify a specialty-grade freeze-dried (not spray-dried) instant — spray-dried tastes like a diner. This is the #1 driver of whether people repurchase. |
| **L-Theanine** | 200 mg | **A** | The real cognitive workhorse. Caffeine + theanine is the best-supported nootropic combination in existence. |
| **Citicoline (Cognizin®)** | 250 mg | **A** | *Recommended over Alpha-GPC here.* Better human RCT data specifically for attention/focus at 250–500 mg, non-hygroscopic (Alpha-GPC is notoriously moisture-grabbing and will clump a powder), and the branded form comes with a substantiation dossier. Keep Alpha-GPC for the pre-workout where power output is the endpoint. |
| **Lion's mane** (fruiting body, ≥30% beta-glucan) | 1,000 mg | **B/C** | Honest picture: a 2025 systematic review of five RCTs found a weighted mean improvement of ~1.17 MMSE points, concentrated in people with mild cognitive impairment — while trials in healthy young adults have shown no acute benefit, and one even showed slightly worse delayed word recall. Effects, where present, build over weeks. **Include it** (consumers expect it, the NGF mechanism is real and interesting) but hang your claims on caffeine + theanine + citicoline, and describe lion's mane in mechanism/tradition language, not outcome promises. |
| **Cordyceps militaris** (fruiting body) | 1,000 mg | C | Endurance/VO2 data is thin and mostly in older or untrained subjects. Ritual ingredient, athlete-coded, fine at an honest dose. |
| **Chaga** | 500 mg | C | Antioxidant/adaptogen expectation. Optional. ⚠️ Chaga is high in oxalates — include a note advising caution for anyone with kidney stone history. |
| MCT powder (C8) | 1–2 g | — | Creaminess without dairy; keto-compatible. Makes it taste expensive. |
| Ceylon cinnamon / cocoa (optional flavor) | — | — | |

**Deliberately excluded:** reishi (sedating — belongs in DEN, not AM), and "proprietary mushroom blend" labeling of any kind. Every mushroom gets its own declared milligram.

### A DEN crossover you should build

**DEN RITUAL · Reishi Night Cacao** — the mushroom line's evening SKU: reishi 1,000 mg + the Night Cacao flavor base + theanine, no caffeine. Reishi's traditional positioning is evening/calm, and it lets your mushroom line span the full day exactly like your pouches do. Same day-to-night shelf story, second use occasion, near-zero new development cost.

| Ingredient | Dose | Grade | Notes |

# PART III — Cross-Cutting Notes

### The full-day product map (your ad strategy in one line)

| Time | Pouch | Powder |
|---|---|---|
| Morning | CHARGE | RITUAL Mushroom Coffee / Matcha |
| Midday | STEADY | — |
| Training | TUNDRA (minerals) | Pre-workout / Creatine |
| Night | **DEN** | **DEN NIGHTCAP** + Reishi Night Cacao |

No competitor in the pouch category owns the night. Own it first and the whole 24-hour system becomes the brand story rather than a list of SKUs.

### Claims discipline (applies to every PM SKU)

Safe: *supports sleep onset · supports relaxation · helps you wind down · supports sleep quality.*
Forbidden: *treats insomnia · cures sleeplessness · sedative · replaces sleep medication · anything naming a condition.*

Every DEN label needs: "Do not drive or operate machinery after use." "Not for use by children, or those pregnant or nursing." "Consult a physician if you take sedatives, psychiatric medication, or anticoagulants." That last one is doing real work for your audience.

### Manufacturing notes for your co-man conversations

- **Glycine at 3 g/serving** means DEN Nightcap is a stick-pack or scoop product, ~10–13 g fill. Fine for any powder co-manufacturer; just confirm stick-pack capability if you want single-serves (recommended — premium unit economics and better for trainer sampling).
- **Alpha-GPC is hygroscopic** — if you ever use it in a powder, specify the 50%-on-carrier form (e.g. AlphaSize 50P). This is why citicoline is the better pick for the AM powders.
- **Branded ingredients (Cognizin®, affron®, Magtein®, Creapure®) cost more but come with clinical dossiers you can cite** — for a small brand building a substantiation file, that's worth the premium on 2–3 hero ingredients. Don't brand everything; brand what you headline.
- **Melatonin is a controlled/restricted supplement ingredient in several countries** (UK, Australia, parts of the EU) — if you ever plan international sales, the melatonin-free SKU becomes your export version. Nice side benefit of building it anyway.
