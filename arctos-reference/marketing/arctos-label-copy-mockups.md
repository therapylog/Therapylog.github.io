## Copy Voice Rules (all SKUs)

1. Short declaratives. The bear doesn't beg. ("One ingredient. Nothing else.")
2. Every claim is structure/function-safe: *supports focus, supports sleep onset, supports hydration* — never treats, cures, manages, or any condition name (no "ADHD," no "insomnia").
3. The COA QR appears on every unit, always framed the same way: **"Every lot third-party tested. Verify yours."** — this sentence is the brand.
4. Negative space is the luxury cue: one product name, one benefit line, one strength number per panel. Resist adding more.
