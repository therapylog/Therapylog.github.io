How to install it on your phone
iPhone / iPad: open therapylog.app/app in Safari (Safari only), tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
Android: open it in Chrome and tap Install app, or menu → Add to Home screen.
There's no APK to sideload and nothing to re-download later — the app updates itself, so you're always on the current version. Your license key arrives by email; enter it once under AI → Settings → Activate a license (a web purchase usually activates itself when you land back in the app). New here? The user guide walks through the whole app.
Install it from your browser — there's no store listing. Add TherapyLog to your home screen from Safari on iPhone or Chrome on Android and it works like any other app, offline included. Staying off the App Store and Play Store is deliberate: it's what lets the app keep the full compound and dosing detail instead of cutting it to pass a review policy.
