Common questions

What's the difference between App Store and Direct APK?
The App Store version updates automatically and integrates with Apple Health. The Direct APK is a one-time Android purchase — you own it forever but install updates manually when we notify you by email. Both have identical features.
Is my payment information secure?
Yes. All payments are processed by Stripe — one of the most trusted payment processors in the world. Your card details never touch our servers. We only see that a payment was completed.
How do I install the Android APK?
After purchase you'll receive an email with the APK file and step-by-step instructions. On your Android device go to Settings → Security → enable "Install from unknown sources", then open the downloaded file to install. Takes about 2 minutes.
Can I cancel my subscription?
Yes, anytime. For App Store subscriptions cancel through your iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. For Google Play cancel in the Play Store. No cancellation fees, no questions asked.
Is there a free trial?
TherapyLog has a free tier that includes core logging and encyclopedia access. The subscription unlocks AI features, clinical tools, and advanced analytics. Download the app first and try the free tier before subscribing.
