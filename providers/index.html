How verification works

What a listing does and does not mean

We check every application before a business appears, and we turn some down. But be clear about what a listing is: it is paid advertising. The check below is a floor, not an endorsement, and higher tiers buy better placement — not a higher standard.

1

You apply

Submit your application with documentation — the state licence or board certification for your practice.

2

We verify

We check your licence or certification number against the issuing body's public register and confirm it is active and unrestricted. That is the whole check. We do not test products, audit facilities, or assess clinical quality.

3

You go live

Approved listings appear in the searchable directory, marked with what was checked and marked as a paid listing.

4

You earn

Earn 30% recurring commission on every TherapyLog Pro subscriber you refer. Separate from your listing fee, and not tied to patient volume.
