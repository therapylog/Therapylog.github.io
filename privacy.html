Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

The short version: Your health data stays on your device. We don't have servers storing your information. The only time data leaves your phone is when you use the AI features — and even then it's not stored by us.

Overview

TherapyLog is built on a privacy-first foundation. All health data you enter — medications, lab results, body measurements, symptoms, blood pressure readings, and personal profile information — is stored exclusively on your device using iOS local storage.

We do not maintain user accounts. We do not store your health data on any server. We do not sell or share your data with third parties. We do not use your data for advertising.

Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your iPhone or iPad only:

  • Personal profile (name, date of birth, sex, health goals)
  • Physician and clinic information
  • Medication and injection logs
  • Bloodwork and lab results (50+ markers)
  • Blood pressure readings
  • Body weight, composition, and measurements
  • Symptom and side effect logs
  • Progress photo notes and check-ins
  • Menstrual cycle entries (female users)
  • Protocol and cycle planner data
  • Reminder times and preferences

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it or use the AI features described below.

AI Features and Data Processing

When you use the AI Research Assistant or the Lab Scanner (camera-to-form feature), data is sent to Anthropic — the maker of Claude AI — for processing:

  • AI Research Assistant: Your question and relevant health context (active protocol, recent lab values, body composition summary) is sent to Anthropic's API to generate a response.
  • Lab Scanner: The photo you capture or upload is sent to Anthropic's API to identify lab values.

This data is processed under Anthropic's Privacy Policy. By default, Anthropic does not retain API data for model training. TherapyLog does not store any AI conversation data on its own servers — none exist.

To opt out of AI processing: Simply do not use the AI Research Assistant or Lab Scanner. All other features work entirely offline with no data transmission.

Apple Health Integration

If you choose to import Apple Health data (HRV, steps, sleep, heart rate, blood pressure readings), this data is read from Apple Health and stored locally within TherapyLog on your device. It is not transmitted to any external service.

TherapyLog requests read-only access to Apple Health data. We do not write data back to Apple Health.

Data Export

When you use the Export feature (Settings → Backup → Export), a JSON file containing your data is saved to your device's Files app. You have complete control over this file. TherapyLog does not receive a copy of your export.

Calendar Export

When you export a dose reminder calendar (.ics file), this file is saved to your device and imported into your iOS Calendar app. No calendar data is transmitted to TherapyLog or any external server.

Analytics and Crash Reporting

TherapyLog does not use any analytics, tracking, or crash reporting services. We collect no usage data, no device identifiers, and no behavioral data of any kind.

Children's Privacy

TherapyLog is intended for adults 17 years of age and older. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from minors.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this privacy policy, we will update this page and note the change in the App Store release notes. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
