Why this matters clinically: lab values in isolation are often misleading. A testosterone of 700 ng/dL looks identical whether the patient was doing 70mg/week or 200mg/week, whether they were 90% adherent or 60%, and whether their E2 was managed or at 55 pg/mL three weeks earlier. TherapyLog shows the trajectory and the context — not just the snapshot.
AI capabilities

A research assistant that knows their protocol.

The AI isn't a generic health chatbot. It receives the user's full health context — active compounds, recent labs, profile, protocol history — and searches PubMed and clinical literature in real time.
AI Research Assistant · grounded in clinical literature

When a user asks "why is my E2 still high on 0.5mg anastrozole twice a week?" — the AI doesn't give a generic answer. It has their specific labs (E2 trending 48 → 52 pg/mL), their current anastrozole dose and frequency, their testosterone dose, their SHBG level, and it searches recent PubMed literature on anastrozole resistance and polymorphisms before responding. It cites the study.

This is the level of answer your audience is currently coming to your videos to find. TherapyLog gives them that same depth, personalized to their exact data, at 2am when they're reading their lab results.
