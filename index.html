Pharmacokinetic Levels

See estimated serum levels for everything you log — modeled per compound from published half-lives and time-to-peak, the way esters actually behave. Watch long esters stack toward steady state, see exactly how long a compound stays active after your last dose, and know when it has cleared. Every half-life is adjustable, and 60+ compounds are pre-loaded.

🧪

Combined Syringe Builder

Plan multi-compound draws: pick compounds, doses, and vial concentrations, and see the exact fill level on your syringe — color-coded per compound. Built-in compatibility rules warn you when compounds shouldn't share a syringe: oil and water never mix, suspensions draw alone, and fragile proteins like HGH degrade in a blend. One tap logs the whole injection.

🛟

Side-Effect Response Guide

High prolactin, estrogen swings, rising hematocrit, early gyno signs, blood pressure, lipids, HPTA recovery — what each one looks like, which labs confirm it, and the responses commonly used in the literature, with the cautions that matter. Harm reduction for people who take their bloodwork seriously.

AI Research Assistant

Ask anything about your protocol, a compound, your labs, training, nutrition, or injury rehab. Gets full context from your logged data. Every answer is grounded in real research — PubMed citations, clinical references, and practitioner knowledge, clearly tiered by evidence quality. 145 questions/month included with Pro, or bring your own key for unlimited.

AI Lab Scanner

Take a photo of your lab report. The AI reads every value and fills in all 50+ fields automatically. Works with Quest, LabCorp, and most clinic report formats. The AI then flags any values that are out of range or trending in the wrong direction — so you know what to ask your provider about.

130+ Compound Encyclopedia

Research-backed reference for TRT, peptides, GLP-1s, AAS, SARMs, senolytics, thyroid, and longevity compounds — 130+ entries across 30 classifications. This is the deepest per-entry reference in any tracker: mechanism, documented dosing ranges, pros and cons, interactions, required monitoring, and live regulatory status — including the FDA's 2026 compounding votes on BPC-157, TB-500, and more.

Clinical Reports

Generate a professional clinical summary covering your active protocol, recent labs, blood pressure history, adherence, and body composition — formatted for a physician appointment. Built for the conversation between patient and provider, not to replace it.

📊

Bloodwork Trends

Track 50+ lab markers with age- and sex-adjusted reference ranges and color-coded optimal zones. Visualize trends over time so you can see how your protocol is affecting your panels. The AI uses this data to give personalized context when you ask about your labs.

💉

Dose Calculator

Full reconstitution calculator with visual syringe diagram. Enter your vial size, BAC water, and target dose — get the exact draw in units and mL. Includes vial planner, cost-per-injection math, and a supply tracker. Never miscalculate a peptide dose or run dry without warning.

AI Training & Rehab Plans

The AI builds training programs and injury-rehabilitation plans grounded in evidence-based methods — periodization, progressive loading, mobility work, return-to-training progressions. It knows your protocol and can factor compounds into your programming. Ask it to build a plan around your goals, equipment, and injury history.

Female Hormone Tracking

Menstrual cycle tracking integrated with your hormone protocol data. Phase guide with protocol timing guidance for women on BHRT, GLP-1, peptides, or other protocols. Automatically enabled for female users. Symptom logging, body composition, and bloodwork all correlate with your cycle phase.

Smart Dose Reminders

Personalized dose reminders with injection site rotation guidance. Tracks your last injection site so you always know where to pin next. Install to your home screen for real push reminders (iOS 16.4+ and Android), or push your protocol schedule to Apple Calendar and Google Calendar in one tap.
