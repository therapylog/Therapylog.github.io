06 AI Assistant

TherapyLog's AI research assistant is powered by Anthropic's Claude API. When you submit a query to the AI assistant, your message — which may include health context from your profile — is transmitted to Anthropic's servers for processing.

What is sent: Your message, relevant profile context (such as your logged protocol or lab data, if you have enabled this), and a system prompt describing TherapyLog's purpose. Your full data history is not transmitted.

Anthropic's data handling: Queries sent to the AI assistant are subject to Anthropic's Privacy Policy. TherapyLog does not store AI query content on its own servers.

AI limitations: AI assistant responses are informational only. They may be incomplete, inaccurate, or outdated. Do not make any health, medication, or treatment decision based solely on an AI response. The AI assistant does not know your complete medical history and is not a substitute for professional medical judgment.

If you use your own Anthropic API key (BYOK mode), your queries are billed to and governed by your own Anthropic account.
